The purpose of this act is to define standards and procedures for owning and operating vehicles in Fuhai. 
# Article 1 - Registration Procedures
## Section 1 - Paperwork
In order to register a vehicle, the owner must file a VM.1.1, which outlines the specifications of the vehicle, its purpose, and its ownership status. A VM.1.1 must include the following information:
1. Stated model of the vehicle
2. Mode of transport (air, land, sea, tunnel bore, orbital)
3. Exact dimensions
4. Maximum vertical speed
5. Maximum horizontal speed
6. Purpose
7. Current legal owner, as per Fuhai property law
A VM.1.1 must be provided in a format that can be easily signed, and it must be verifiable that the document was signed.
## Section 2 - Verification
In order for a vehicle to pass its registration, a member of government must examine the vehicle to determine if it matches the specifications listed on the VM.1.1 filed. If any information is found to be incorrect or fraudulent, the VM.1.1 will be denied. The VM.1.1 will also be denied if it is not allowable under Section 4. 
## Section 3 - Final Registration
If the vehicle passes the inspection and verification, the VM.1.1 shall be signed by the presiding member of government, and a copy will be given to the owner of the vehicle. The original shall be kept in the Government Building library. The signed VM.1.1 acts as registration for the vehicle, allowing it to be used by the owner for the listed purpose. The signed VM.1.1 will be named exactly as the issued registration ID for the vehicle.
## Section 4 - Allowable Vehicles
Vehicles can be denied registration for several reasons. They are as follows:
1. The purpose of the vehicle is combat or non-consenting destruction
2. The purpose of the vehicle is to facilitate illegal activity
3. The vehicle is not in need of registration
4. The owner of the vehicle is currently in prison
5. The owner of the vehicle is currently missing or otherwise unavailable for communication
6. The vehicle is capable of mass destruction
7. The vehicle is larger than 200 blocks in any direction
## Section 5 - Vehicles that Do Not Require Registration
For a vehicle to not require registration, it must fit at least one of the following criteria:
1. The vehicle is less than 4 blocks tall, wide, and deep
2. The vehicle has a volume of less than 9 blocks
3. The vehicle does not contain any moving parts
# Article 2 - Obligations of Vehicle Operators
## Section 1 - Ownership
There is no need to register a vehicle in order to own it. Simply storing an unregistered vehicle is permitted. If you do own a vehicle that is registered, you are required to display  the registration tag showing text that matches exactly the title of the signed VM.1.1. Possessing a registered vehicle without displaying the registration ID would be against this law.
## Section 2 - Operation
In order to legally operate a vehicle (i.e., use it for transportation), you must have it registered as long as it is a vehicle that requires registration. Operating a vehicle without a registration in Fuhai land and airspace is against the law. You cannot operate a vehicle while under the influence of any mind-altering drugs.
# Article 3 - Legal Action when In Breach of the Law
## Section 1 - Operating a Vehicle Without a Registration
It is illegal to operate a vehicle without a registration if that vehicle requires a registration under VM.1.5. Doing so is a Section 1 offence, and leads you liable to the following:
1. Maximum 100Fi in fines per infraction
## Section 2 - Operation a Vehicle Under the Influence
It is illegal to operate a vehicle while under the influence of mind-altering drugs. Doing so is a Section 2 offence, and leads you liable to the following:
1. Maximum 300Fi in fines per infraction
2. 5 hours of mandatory community service
3. Maximum 5 days mandatory confinement
## Section 3 - Owning a Registered Vehicle Without Proper Identification
It is illegal to own a registered vehicle without an identifying tag plainly visible that lists the exact registration ID of the vehicle. Doing so is a Section 1 offence, and leads you liable to the following:
1. Maximum 50Fi in fines per infraction
